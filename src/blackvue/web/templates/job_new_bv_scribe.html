Describe scenes (bv-scribe)

Runs a vision-language model over one camera's archive to describe what's happening in each recording and/or read visible text (signs, plates) via OCR - the same work bv-scribe does from the command line, with full parity for every flag it supports. The Advanced sections below hold the same fine-tuning knobs bv-scribe --help lists up front (frame sampling, the zoom-detection sub-pipeline) - the CLI has no choice but to show everything at once, but here they stay out of the way until you actually want them, and the trip summary knob opens on its own once its checkbox above is checked.
