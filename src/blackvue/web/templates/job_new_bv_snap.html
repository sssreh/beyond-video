Snapshot (bv-snap)

Grabs one live JPEG snapshot per camera direction (Front/Rear/ Interior by default) over the network and saves each to ~/beyond-video-data/snapshots/<camera> on this server. A direction that fails, or that a camera model doesn't support (Interior support in particular is unconfirmed on some hardware), is simply skipped rather than failing the whole snap - check the job output for which directions actually saved.
