Export trips (bv-export)

Groups one camera's archive into trips and writes each one's front/rear video, audio, GPS track, and subtitles into its own folder - the same work bv-export does from the command line, with full parity for every flag it supports. Exported trips show up on the Trips tab as soon as this job finishes. The destination folder isn't configurable here - it's always this server's own trips directory. The Advanced sections below hold the same options bv-export --help lists up front - the CLI has no choice but to show everything at once, but here they stay out of the way until you actually want them, and open on their own the moment a related checkbox above them is checked.
