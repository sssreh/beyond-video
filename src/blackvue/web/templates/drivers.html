Assign a driver for a date range

For a stretch of days where you already know who was driving - e.g. "only I was driving, my wife was out of town for 4 days" - set one driver for every undecided trip in that range in a single save. Trips already resolved by a common-place rule or an automatic pattern match are left alone; only trips still shown as undecided below are affected.

Specific trips

Trips with no resolved driver at all - a one-off destination, or a common place above you haven't filled in yet. Assign a driver to just this trip, or leave it undecided. Smoothness (0=smoothest, 9=most aggressive) ranks this trip's g-sensor readings against every other trip's own - a relative scale, not a fixed threshold - and "most similar decided trip" points at the closest-matching trip you've already assigned, as a hint toward who was probably driving. Use the filter below to switch from Undecided (the default) to a specific driver, to review or correct trips already resolved to them.
